Melech designs custom interiors, builds them in its own workshop and installs them: kitchens, bedrooms, children's rooms, built-in wardrobes, doors and wall panelling. The company has more than 30 years of craftsmanship behind it.

## The brief

The original website no longer reflected the quality of the company's work. The goal was a complete redesign: a modern, professional presence that quickly shows visitors what Melech offers and leads them to a free consultation.

## The approach

I redesigned the site from the ground up with a focus on clean visuals, bold typography and large photos of finished projects. The content is organised into clear sections (services, craft and design, projects, about) and the call to book a free consultation is always within reach. The site runs on WordPress, so its content is easy to keep up to date.
