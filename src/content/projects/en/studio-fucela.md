Štúdio Fúčela helps companies simplify processes, clarify responsibilities and use automation and AI where it truly pays off.

## The brief

The original website needed reworked content and visuals to communicate the studio's services clearly and credibly, and to work well on mobile.

## The approach

We reworked and redesigned the site with a focus on readable typography, calm colours and clear calls to action. Services are clearly organised and the site runs on WordPress, so its content is easy to keep up to date.
